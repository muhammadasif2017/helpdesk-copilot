# Accounts & Access

## Password resets
Customers reset passwords via the "Forgot password" link; the email arrives
within 5 minutes. If it never arrives, check the account's email spelling in
the admin panel and ask them to check spam. Support agents must never set a
password manually or read one over any channel.

## Account lockouts
Five failed login attempts lock an account for 30 minutes. Agents can unlock
early from the admin panel after verifying identity with the last 4 digits of
the payment card and the billing ZIP code.

## Account deletion
Deletion requests are handled under privacy regulations: verify identity, then
file a deletion ticket with the privacy team. Deletion completes within 30
days and is irreversible. Order history required for open disputes is retained
as allowed by law.

## Identity verification
Never verify identity by asking for a full card number or password. Acceptable
factors: registered email access, last 4 digits of card, billing ZIP, or order
number plus delivery address.

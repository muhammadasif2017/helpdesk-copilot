# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Footwear and apparel have an extended 45-day return window. Items must be
unworn and in original packaging.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, personalized items, and clearance items marked "final sale" cannot
be returned. Opened hygiene products (earbuds, cosmetics) are also
non-returnable unless defective.

## Refund processing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of
the warehouse receiving the return. If a customer reports a missing refund
after 10 business days, escalate to the billing team.

## Double charges
If a customer reports being charged twice for one order, verify both charges in
the payment dashboard. If one is a pending authorization, it will drop off
within 3 business days — inform the customer. If both charges settled, file a
billing escalation; do not promise a timeline beyond "3–5 business days".

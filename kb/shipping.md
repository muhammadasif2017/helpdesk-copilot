# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business
days. Orders placed before 2pm local warehouse time ship the same day.

## Lost or missing packages
If tracking shows "delivered" but the customer reports the package missing,
ask them to check with neighbors and the building mailroom first. If still
missing after 24 hours, file a carrier claim and offer either a replacement
shipment or a full refund — customer's choice. Do not require a police report.

## Address changes
The shipping address can be changed only before the order status reaches
"packed". After that, advise the customer to refuse delivery or start a return
once it arrives.

## International shipping
We ship to the US, Canada, UK, and EU. Customs duties for international orders
are the customer's responsibility and are not refundable by us.
